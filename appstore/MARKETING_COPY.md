## English (US) — Primary language

### Subtitle (29 / 30)
Screenshot history, instantly

### Promotional text (133 / 170)
Every screenshot, saved the moment you take it. Browse your full history from the menu bar and re-copy any past capture in one click.

### Description
Your screenshot history, always ready on the clipboard.

ClipShot lives quietly in your menu bar and saves every screenshot you take, automatically. Forget hunting through Desktop, Downloads, or that one folder you swore you'd organize. Open the menu, see a clean visual history grouped by month, and click any thumbnail to copy it straight back to the clipboard.

Designed to feel like a part of macOS — not a layer on top of it. ClipShot doesn't change your system settings, doesn't intercept the screenshot tool, and doesn't ask you to learn new shortcuts. Use Cmd+Shift+3, 4 or 5 just like you always have. ClipShot picks up from there.

What you can do:

- Every screenshot, saved — captures appear in your history within seconds.
- Re-copy with one click — past screenshots flow straight back to the clipboard.
- Floating thumbnail — click to edit in Markup, or drag straight into any app.
- Organized by month — history grouped in folders like January 2026.
- Open at login (optional) — always ready, never in the way.
- Pick your own folder — Desktop, Downloads, or anywhere you save captures.
- Clear history anytime — remove everything, or just items older than 30 days.

🔒 Privacy, by design. ClipShot runs 100% on your Mac. No accounts. No servers. No telemetry. Nothing is ever sent to the internet — not a single byte. Your screenshots are yours, and only yours.

Take a screenshot — it's already saved.
